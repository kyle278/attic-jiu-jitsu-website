# Offer Brief: Attic Beginner Programme

Prepared for: Attic Jiu Jitsu, Carlow
Mode: Build, using the existing published offer rather than changing commercial terms.
Assumptions: prospective adults may worry about experience, equipment, schedule and first-visit uncertainty. These are planning hypotheses, not supplied customer interview findings. Value-equation scores below are qualitative design judgments. No price or delivery-cost assumptions are introduced. Component monetary values, capacity, payment timing and refund policies are unknown. All prices quoted are the checked catalogue snapshot and implementation must use the live read-only catalogue.

## 1. Market and avatar

**Avatar:** An adult in or near Carlow who is curious about Brazilian jiu jitsu, has no experience and wants a structured way to begin with a local coach. Their immediate purchasing problem is understanding which class to attend and what equipment and membership they need. This is a website target hypothesis; parents and experienced students remain supported on separate routes.

| Test | Pass / Fail | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Massive pain | Not established | Beginner uncertainty is plausible; no interview evidence supports severe pain. Do not intensify it with fear copy. |
| Purchasing power | Not established | Published €160 / 2 months programme proves an offer exists, not local affordability or demand. |
| Easy to target | Provisional pass | Geographic location and existing social channels give a practical local audience definition. No audience-size claim. |
| Growing | Unknown | No local category growth evidence in the research pack. |

**Niche decision:** Make adult beginners the main homepage conversion route because Attic has a specific published package for them. Do not raise prices or exclude other customers based on the method's premium-price argument. The evidence does not establish a starving crowd or justify new commercial terms.

## 2. Dream outcome

**In their words:** “I want to know how to start jiu jitsu and feel comfortable walking into my first class.” This is proposed copy language, not a customer quotation.
**Status version:** Become someone who has started learning a demanding skill with a local team. Do not promise confidence, weight loss, competition success or social status as an outcome.

## 6. Pricing and terms

- **Price anchor:** Concrete contents and duration, not a fictional sum of kit and class retail values. The live Wix saving percentage is excluded because its comparison basis is unclear.
- **Total stated value:** Monetary total unverified. Never present a manufactured value-to-price multiple.
- **Price:** Current catalogue €160 / 2 months for Adults Beginners program. No calculated value ratio.
- **Why this price:** Existing published commercial term; this brief improves clarity and makes no repricing decision.
- **Terms:** Eight weeks of training, unlimited beginner classes, kimono, white belt, bag; plan valid two months. Payment timing, size availability, renewal/cancellation, refund terms and start-date rules require academy confirmation. Enquiry is not booking. No incentive to act today is known. The visitor must contact the team and participate in training; no minimum attendance or result promise is invented.
- **Tiering (optional):** Keep actual catalogue alternatives, including adult €69/month Membership, student €50/month and kids/family plans. Do not invent a premium tier or restricted supply. Family coverage must be confirmed with academy.

## 7. Scarcity and urgency

- **Scarcity:** None claimed. Capacity is unknown. Future mechanism only if academy supplies an enforced class/cohort limit; do not publish placeholder counts.
- **Urgency:** None claimed. No verified start deadline or expiring price. Honest reason to enquire: “Ask the team which first class suits you.”

## 11. The offer in one paragraph (as the prospect would read it)

New to jiu jitsu? Start with the Attic adult beginner programme in Carlow. The current programme includes eight weeks of unlimited beginner classes, a kimono, white belt and bag, for €160, with the plan valid for two months. Meet the coaches, view the timetable and send an enquiry to find the right first class. The academy will confirm your start date and next steps directly.
