# Attic website research and shared evidence

Research date: 27 October 2026. This is the shared evidence pack for all three design agents. Use ONLY this research, the current repository, and the live atticbjj.net site as references for flow, copy, colours and fonts. Skills provide a method, not another brand to copy. Do not conduct independent inspiration research or introduce unrelated visual identities.

## Scope and evidence rules

Rebuild the public marketing website in this existing Next 16.2 / React 19 project. Preserve the booking system byte for byte: src/app/members/**, src/app/api/**, src/components/members/**, gym-member-portal.tsx, src/lib/**, src/generated/**, public/members/**, scripts that support members. Leave root layout and globals.css unchanged; all new styles must be scoped below .website-shell in the website route group. Existing public catalogue functions may be consumed read-only. Public classes/timetable and memberships pages may be redesigned, without altering data, availability or booking behaviour. Keep existing routes and anchor destinations. Member entrance is /members.

No new claims, fabricated testimonials, invented review counts, fake availability, promises of fitness outcomes, discounts, guarantees, or free trials without evidence. Existing project says free trial, but live Wix only says try a class; use Start training / Plan your first class with enquiry wording so we do not promise an unverified free service or confirmed reservation. Catalogue is the public price source. Do not replace it with static marketing prices.

## 2. BJJ club benchmarks: what works and what transfers

[Academy](https://artofjiujitsu.com/pages/academy), [programme story](https://artofjiujitsu.com/pages/academy-2023), [trial selector](https://artofjiujitsu.com/pages/book-trial-class), [membership flow](https://artofjiujitsu.com/pages/join-academy).

Observed: adult/kids/no-gi/private programme structure, owned academy photography, named locations, trial and membership entrances. The trial flow asks location/programme/level and explains basics versus fundamentals. Membership discloses residency rules and uniform charges. Its positioning connects technical study with personal progress. Transfer: a clear adult-beginner/experienced/youth route, specific equipment disclosures and coach evidence. Do not copy its high-end exclusivity or locality gates to an inclusive Carlow academy. Marketing evidence is the visible trial acquisition funnel and academy/education/retail ecosystem, not a verified paid-media strategy.

### Grange Jiu Jitsu, West London

[Homepage](https://grangejiujitsu.com/).

Observed: immediate beginner reassurance, named black-belt coaches, parent and member reviews, different adult/youth/women routes, displayed membership entry point, local transport information and repeated free-class CTA. Programme choice sits alongside proof and practical visit information. Transfer: answer experience/fitness worries, show the actual coaches, provide a clear address and class path. Use Attic's own evidence only; none of Grange's testimonials, star ratings or offers belongs on Attic. Marketing evidence: location-specific search copy, public Google review integration, merch, free-trial entry route. The [official blog](https://grangejiujitsu.com/blog/) also publishes beginner articles on first-class expectations and self-defence, demonstrating educational search/content marketing. These demonstrate channels/patterns, not measured conversions.

### New School BJJ Brixton

[Homepage](https://www.newschoolbjjlondon.co.uk/).

### New School network

### Aurum BJJ, Dublin

[Homepage](https://aurumbjjdublin.com/).

## 4. Advanced motion references and engineering lessons

### basement.studio

[Homepage](https://basement.studio/), [Daylight case study](https://basement.studio/showcase/daylight-simplicity-in-motion).

Browser-inspected navigation/content: clear services, featured projects and contact remain discoverable around a motion-led identity; music is opt-in via a control. Official case study describes a story-driven, humanised product launch and performance focus. Transfer: a single choreographed opening and strong art-directed imagery, with reliable conventional navigation. Studio claims of commercial success are self-reported and do not establish a causal animation conversion lift. Do not copy the studio's tone, novelty navigation or graphics.

### Lusion

[Homepage](https://lusion.co/), [about](https://lusion.co/about/).

Official site describes 3D storytelling, motion and immersive interactive work with project examples. It is a useful upper bound on motion ambition, not a direct small-gym UX template. Transfer: one memorable scene, depth through layered photographic movement, carefully paced entrances. Reject a heavy 3D loading sequence, audio autoplay, cursor replacement and scroll hijacking. No evidence that this class of motion increases local-gym enquiries.

### Motion implementation conclusion

Attic should feel like deliberate athletic movement: a 700–900ms staggered hero type/image entrance; a restrained desktop-only scroll-driven photo translation/scale or composition shift; small arrow/underline feedback on navigation; 180–220ms state changes for tabs/menu/FAQ; optional editorial photo strips. No fade-slide on every section. Content must be server-rendered and visible before JS, never wait on motion to see an action. prefers-reduced-motion disables transforms/scroll scenes; mobile gets static photo compositions and normal scrolling. No infinite automatic carousels or text marquees without pause controls. Use a small isolated animation controller with cleanup rather than turning the entire site into a client component.

## 5. Recommended shared direction

Brand idea: serious jiu jitsu, a place for everyone. Keep Attic's red/ink/chalk and existing fonts. Black-and-white authentic photography, flat surfaces, sharp geometry inspired by mat boundaries and belt stripes, calm body copy. Alternate dark photographic moments with open chalk editorial sections so the whole page is not endless black cards. Hero should identify Brazilian jiu jitsu, Carlow and beginner welcome immediately, with one primary Start training action and a secondary View timetable action. Red is a clear action colour, not decorative haze. No gradients on buttons, glowing cards or repeated labelled eyebrow/card/CTA stacks.

Home flow candidate: local identity + welcoming photographic hero; compact verified proof strip; audience/programme selector; adult starter offer using read-only catalogue, with exact price/term/gear; coaching; real training photo story; FAQ; location and enquiry. Existing members get a distinct /members link in header and footer, without changing the app. Inner pages provide real detail, not copies of home.

## 6. Advertising translation for Attic (proposals, not executed campaigns)

The current evidence supports a beginner starter pack, family coaching, authentic training photos, social accounts and local presence. It does NOT establish current CAC, conversion rate, ad budget, capacity, response time, active paid ads, review rights or attribution quality. Do not fabricate those.

Candidate acquisition system: coach-led educational social clips; answer common first-class questions; photos of real training with permission; occasional clear beginner invitation; local search landing clarity and correct contact details; personal referrals without inventing rewards; tightly scoped local paid experiments only after budget and capacity are supplied. Preserve existing Ingenium tracker, form attributes and UTM fields. Measure enquiry success, class interest, membership interest and timetable clicks, then attendance and membership in the existing business workflow. No ad launch, outreach or message sending is authorised by this build request.

Website job: make the promise credible, reduce first-visit uncertainty, show what the starter includes, and provide a clear human response path. Enquiry success is not a booked class. Display that distinction in UI copy.

## 7. Required agent process

Each agent writes a detailed independent website plan v1 including full page architecture, flow, sample copy, colours/fonts tied to evidence, desktop/mobile layouts, UI states, UX, motion timing/trigger/fallback, technical scope, accessibility and verification. Do not edit source code. Each owns separate files. Root will then coordinate exactly TWO cross-critique/revision rounds: every agent critiques BOTH peers, reads feedback from BOTH, and revises its own plan (v2 then v3). Critique must state actionable weaknesses and what to keep. After round two, root compiles a build brief and implements. A final implementation review is separate from these two design revision rounds.
