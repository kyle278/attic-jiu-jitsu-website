## 2. Lead magnet (or core offer decision)

- **Decision:** Present the existing core beginner offer first. Provide useful first-visit guidance publicly instead of adding a gated lead magnet that makes visitors submit before learning price and schedule.
- **Narrow problem it solves:** “How do I know where to start?” Answer class choice, equipment, location and enquiry process. Next problem is beginning sustained coached training, served by the actual programme.
- **Type:** One step of a multi-step process, as public information; not a promised free class.
- **Delivery vehicle:** Information on Classes and FAQ; future coach video only if it is actually recorded and captioned.
- **Name candidates to test:** “Plan your first class”, “New to jiu jitsu?”, “Your first class at Attic”. Proposed informal feedback test with existing members before a traffic experiment; no claim of a winning name.
- **Consumption plan:** Mobile readable, no account or email gate, clear timetable and current offer links. Exact programme details remain in text, not embedded in a social video.
- **CTA:** “Start training” leads to enquiry; helper “Ask us which first class suits you.” The reason is practical help, not a fictitious deadline or scarcity count.

## 4. Playbook

### Content proposal

Audience: Carlow adult beginners on the existing Attic Instagram; repurpose to existing Facebook only when staff can maintain it. Owner: an academy-designated content lead, coach approves factual teaching content. Daily proposed input: one short planning/capture session [assumption: 20 minutes per operating weekday], one useful response to an existing audience question if a question exists [assumption: maximum one planned response daily; do not invent people to contact]. Publish one useful post per weekday [assumption: capacity-dependent starting cadence], using an eight-post cycle [assumption: editorial test]: seven gives and one direct offer ask [assumption: give:ask 7:1]. Link in profile uses the rebuilt site; post text names the exact destination when platform links are limited.

Ready-to-publish educational example after coach approval: “Gi or no-gi? Gi classes use the training kimono. No-gi is grappling without it. Attic has both in Carlow, including beginner routes. If you are choosing your first class, check the class guide or ask the team which session suits you.” Use an actual training photo and accurate caption, no unapproved participant story.

Other gives: demonstrate what the coach calls a basic position; show where the academy entrance is without asserting parking arrangements; explain the included starter kit; introduce Darren or Brian with verified dates; explain how to read the timetable; answer parent age-group questions with confirmation caveat; show how beginner and mixed-level labels differ using published classes. No instructional health claims or fabricated “member transformation”.

Ready-to-publish ask: “Thinking about trying jiu jitsu in Carlow? Attic's adult beginner programme includes eight weeks of unlimited beginner classes, a kimono, white belt and bag. The current plan is €160 and valid for two months. Visit the membership page for current details, then send an enquiry so the team can help you choose your first class.” Check live catalogue before publication because a static post can age.

Follow-up: content lead reviews genuine questions once per operating day [assumption: one check], provides useful answer, and offers relevant page only when helpful. Do not promise an unverified response time publicly. Stop follow-up if person declines. Youth imagery requires existing rights/permission confirmation; do not infer rights from possession of a photo.

### Warm response and referrals proposal

List source: people who have actually enquired and members who voluntarily choose to share. Owner: academy staff. Daily input: review the actual enquiry inbox once per operating day [assumption: one review], record each reply/next step; no required unsolicited send quota. Complete reply example: “Hi [First name], thanks for getting in touch about starting jiu jitsu. Have you trained before, and are you looking for adult, kids or teens classes? You can see our current options and timetable on the website. Once we know which route fits, we can talk through your first class and start date.” The staff member then supplies verified arrangements; never sends an automatic fabricated confirmation.

Voluntary referral ask, used after a real positive member interaction: “If someone you know is curious about jiu jitsu, you're welcome to share our class guide with them. It explains the beginner route and how to ask about a first class.” Do not collect a friend's phone number or offer rewards without policy. Optional follow-up to an unanswered requested enquiry: “Hi [First name], just checking whether you still need help choosing a class at Attic. Happy to answer any questions. If your plans have changed, there is no need to reply.” Proposed maximum one follow-up [assumption: conservative cadence pending academy preference]; stop thereafter unless the person responds.

- **Daily actions:** Proposed capacity test of 20 minutes content work, one weekday post, one inbox review, and at most one relevant reply/follow-up per active conversation [all assumption: quantities require owner capacity]. Seven giving posts precede each direct ask [assumption]. No cold send quota.
- **Hours this costs per day:** Content allocation 20/60 = 0.33 hours [assumption]; response time is variable and must be measured, not guessed.
- **Duration before judging:** Proposed operational review after four weeks [assumption], not a verdict on channel viability. Book benchmark: a new channel can take three to six months. Content cadence should be increased only when it is useful, sustainable and permission-compliant.

## 6. The math

Use observed values instead of a forecast: daily content minutes and posts → attributed sessions → successful enquiries → academy-confirmed first visits → paying members → collected revenue. A CTA click is an intent signal, not an engaged lead. An identifiable enquiry showing interest is an engaged lead. Attendance is confirmed by academy workflow, not inferred from form success.

Let V = attributed sessions, E = successful relevant enquiries, A = confirmed attendance, M = new paying members, R = collected receipts, S = ad spend, L = attributable labour and production costs. Then enquiry rate = E/V; attendance rate = A/E; membership conversion = M/A; CAC = (S+L)/M; LTGP = total receipts minus fulfilment costs over the customer's actual lifetime. If denominator is zero report “not enough data”, not a pleasing ratio. Current E, A, M, R, S, L and retention are unknown, so projected customers and revenue are unavailable.

- **Projected CAC:** Not estimable. **LTGP:CAC:** Not estimable. Book benchmark: 3:1 is a planning health check, not a universal law or Attic target proven by data.
- **30-day cash check:** Current adult starter receipt could be €160 only if paid upfront, but timing is unknown. Do not assume upfront collection or subtract unprovided fulfilment costs. Cash contribution = receipts collected within 30 days minus acquisition and fulfilment paid within 30 days. No immediate upsell or higher price introduced by this rebuild.

## 7. Scale path (More, Better, New)

- **More:** Once capacity and attributed enquiries are measured, increase useful content capture before adding channels. Doubling the proposed 20-minute input to 40 minutes [assumption: optional future test] requires evidence that coaching and enquiry response can support it. Never double outreach to people who did not ask.
- **Better:** Content lead checks weekly relevant enquiry count [assumption: one weekly review] and actual weakest measurable step. Test one headline or matching destination per week [assumption: test cadence], with low traffic favouring qualitative learning over spurious significance. Keep log of date, hypothesis, variant, traffic source, enquiry quality and outcome.
- **New:** New placement within existing Instagram first, then reuse on Facebook, then consider a local paid test after budget/capacity/unit economics are known. No predicted winning placement. Paid execution requires approved creative and budget; it is outside this request.

Staff delegation uses Document, Demonstrate, Duplicate: document the useful-content and enquiry workflow, coach demonstrates it, staff repeats and is observed. No agency or affiliate contract proposed; no evidence that their overhead is appropriate.

- **Roadmap level today:** Unknown; established memberships do not prove a measured acquisition level. **Next level requires:** Document actual inputs, relevant enquiries, attendance and membership outcomes, then choose sustainable capacity. Do not manufacture the book's referral-share threshold as an Attic result.
