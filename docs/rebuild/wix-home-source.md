## WHAT IS JIU JITSU?

Jiu Jitsu is a grappling martial art that focuses on taking your opponent to the ground and applying joint locks and chokes to finish the fight. It's a great workout that will improve your strength, endurance, and flexibility. Our club offers classes for all skill levels, from beginners to advanced students. Our experienced instructors will provide you with the guidance and support you need to succeed. Come and try a class today and see for yourself!
